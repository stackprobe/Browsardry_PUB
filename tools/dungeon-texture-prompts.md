# ダンジョン壁テクスチャのプロンプト

王都・聖都の壁は512×512の不透明PNGとし、各迷宮の意匠に合わせる。

## 王都

- 保存先: `DocRoot/assets/textures/royal-vault.png`
- プロンプト:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: square opaque tileable dungeon wall texture, flat orthographic straight-on game material, no scene perspective. Use dark blue-gray weathered ashlar stone and mortar, edge-to-edge masonry texture and diffuse lighting. Carve ONE prominent heraldic wyvern crest exactly in the center of the entire texture. Wyvern has two legs, bat wings, curved long neck and curled tail, in a compact shield-shaped incised stone relief. It must look engraved deeply into the stone itself, NOT painted, NOT a metal plaque attached, not freestanding creature. Central carving should occupy about 50% of texture width and 60% height, clear readable silhouette in low light. Surrounding stone retains masonry joints, subtle wear; carving may span several blocks. No other emblems, no lettering, no border, no floor or ceiling, no transparency, no cast shadow or vignette at tile edges. Opposite outer edges should remain visually repeatable. Output one square PNG texture.

## 聖都

- 保存先: `DocRoot/assets/textures/sacred-seal.png`
- プロンプト:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: square opaque tileable dungeon wall game texture, flat orthographic straight-on material. Use pale warm ivory limestone, ashlar masonry, worn edges, fine cracks and diffuse lighting. Place ONE clearly readable sacred metal ornament mounted exactly in the center of the WHOLE square texture. Ornament design: an elegant eight-pointed star with the four long cardinal arms forming a cross-like silhouette, four shorter diagonal rays; subtly aged silver with restrained old-gold edging, about 45% texture width and 55% height. It must visibly be a raised object ATTACHED on top of the stone with modest thickness, discreet mounting and gentle contact shadows, NOT engraved or painted. No glow, no text, no gems, no other symbols. Keep outer tile edges plain masonry that can repeat seamlessly; no perspective, no wall frame, no floor, no ceiling, no vignette, no transparent background. Output one square PNG texture.
